# Contents

## Home page

Goal: tell a visitor what the app is.

Structure, in each language:
- the app name, as the page's heading and its document title: `Prêt d'outils` in French,
  `Tool Lending` in English;
- one short paragraph: the app lets neighbourhood associations lend
  tools to their members, and lets anyone browse what they lend;
- in the header, the account link: `Se connecter` in French and
  `Sign in` in English without a signed-in volunteer, `Tableau de
  bord` in French and `Dashboard` in English with one.

## Sign-in page

Goal: let a volunteer sign in.

Structure, in each language:
- the title, as the page's heading and its document title:
  `Connexion` in French, `Sign in` in English;
- the failure message: the login or the password is wrong, or there
  were too many attempts and the volunteer must wait a few minutes;
- the labels of the login and password fields, and of the sign-in
  button.

## Dashboard page

Goal: show a volunteer which association they are signed in to.

Structure, in each language:
- the label of the sign-out button.
