# Overview

A web application for neighbourhood associations in Quebec that lend
tools to their members. One hosted installation serves several
associations, each with its own data. Volunteers register members and
keep a catalogue of tools; anyone on the internet browses the
catalogue. A demo mode with sample data shows the features.

The interface is in French by default, and in English at the
visitor's choice. Member records hold personal data under Quebec's
Law 25.

This version holds the home page, the volunteers' sign-in, and the
volunteers' management of the volunteers and the members of their
association.

## Non-goals

- No loan tracking: who borrowed a tool, or when it is due.
- No member accounts: only volunteers sign in.
- No payments, deposits or fees.
- No emails or notifications.
- No public sign-up: the operator creates each association.
