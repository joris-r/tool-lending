# Contents

## Dashboard page

- the label of the sign-out button;…
- the label of the members link: `Membres` in French, `Members` in
  English;

## Members page

Goal: let a volunteer see the members of their association, and add
one.

Structure, in each language:
- the title, as the page's heading and its document title:
  `Membres` in French, `Members` in English;
- the label of the dashboard link;
- the messages after each operation: the member was added, their
  record was saved, they were deleted;
- the empty-list text: the association has no member yet;
- the label of a member's edit link;
- the heading of the add form, and the labels of its name and contact
  fields and of its add button; the contact label says it may be a
  phone number, an email or an address, and is optional.

## Member page

Goal: let a volunteer correct a member's record, or start deleting
it.

Structure, in each language:
- the label of the members link;
- the labels of the name and contact fields, and of the save button;
- the label of the delete link.

## Member deletion page

Goal: make a volunteer confirm that they delete a member for good.

Structure, in each language:
- the title, as the page's heading and its document title:
  `Supprimer un membre` in French, `Delete a member` in English;
- the question: delete this member, by name; their record cannot be
  recovered afterwards;
- the labels of the confirm button and of the cancel link.
